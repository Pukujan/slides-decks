# Historic visual trials — v0.1.0
Five actual eight-page PDF trials were rendered in an earlier working session. They were **not** compared pixel-for-pixel to the actual golden PDF. These are negative/diagnostic examples, NOT an accepted style library.

| Trial | Purpose | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Basic Folio vocabulary | Repeated composition, broken wrap; fail |
| 02 | Stronger yellow accent control | Minimal substantive improvement; fail |
| 03 | More editorial hierarchy labels | Typography regression and orphan split; fail |
| 04 | Correct wraps and introduce story emphasis | Improves hierarchy but overworked accent spacing; revise |
| 05 | Cleaner spacing and more coherent geometry | Best of five; still mechanically repetitive and **not golden-validated** |

Mechanical checks reported intact PPTX, 8 PDF pages at 8×10 inches, all subheaders, and no off-canvas shape bounding boxes. Yet bad line wrapping and dull compositions persisted. Structural QC and visual/editorial QC **must be separate gates**.

**Mandatory next validation:** inspect actual golden pages, annotate positive/negative page-specific decisions, run at least one true fresh ChatGPT new-story session, and compare targeted regeneration with prior accepted work. Do not infer stylistic fidelity from technical validity.
