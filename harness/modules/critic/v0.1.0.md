# Multi-axis creative critic · v0.1.0
Evaluate independently; a valid PDF is not necessarily a good slide deck.

1. **Truth:** are claims sourced? Are conjecture and illustration metaphors distinguished from fact?
2. **Story:** strong tension, intelligent order, unequal weighting, purposeful reveals, no obligatory eight-beat template.
3. **Voice:** human, precise, not audit-speak, juvenile, boilerplate or sensational without evidence.
4. **Brand:** white/ink/yellow grammar, editorial typography, balanced white space, varied compositions, coherent with *actual* golden PDF.
5. **Illustration:** legible action, identity/stroke consistency, picture enhances rather than repeats copy.
6. **Technical:** correct output type, no clipping, crowding, overflow, missing copy; view at actual mobile size; validate rendered output independently.
7. **Scope:** no unapproved edits, slide count drift, new formats or unrelated regeneration.

**Ticket for every failure:** `id, observed evidence, severity (blocker/major/minor), owning_state, violated_module@version, smallest_fix, affected_artifacts, required_regressions`. Flag uncertainty where actual golden not available.

A critic may inspect and **recommend** a rollback; a critic never rewrites already-approved stages. Route according to `router/rules.v0.1.0.md`, invalidate descendants and seek renewed human approval if changed meaning/plan.

Never award "golden match" on basis of fonts, page count, file validity or geometry alone. Different stories should be reviewed for *brand and editorial fidelity*, not pixel match.
