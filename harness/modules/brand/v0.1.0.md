# Folio brand DNA · v0.1.0 (transcript-grounded, not page-annotated)
**Golden authority:** the user-selected eight-page A–Folio PDF committed in `transcripts/.../attachments/`. The PPTX may not match it; **never learn defects from the PPTX as brand rules**.

**Core grammar**
- White / paper-like field, dark ink text, yellow accent as a small intentional signal. Avoid dark sci-fi dashboard look.
- Editorial typographic contrast: commanding headline, quiet supporting type, compact metadata/index when needed. Clear hierarchy and generous purposeful negative space.
- Composition responds to narrative significance: sometimes a headline owns the page, sometimes the illustration; avoid monotonous left-headline/right-figure repetition.
- Text-image proximity should clarify the same moment. Accents, arrows, and callouts must have meaning. No decorative UI clutter.
- The exemplar is 4:5 portrait (8×10 in), but 4:5 is an output setting, not a universal creative formula.

**Hard limits:** consistency of identity/palette; no generic tech illustration packs, unapproved standalone image generation or screenshots masquerading as editable slides. No preemptive style changes.
**Freedom:** purposeful asymmetry, white-space variation, scale and occasional editorial surprise, *when justified by the story*.

**Evidence caveat:** these are derived from the transcript and recorded generation choices. Final page-by-page annotations of the actual golden PDF remain an open evaluation task; do not claim measured visual fidelity before inspection.
